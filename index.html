Barcode-based blockchain product authentication

How it works

Every product carries a unique barcode tied to an immutable ledger.

  1. Manufacturer registers a product, a unique Code 128 barcode is minted on-chain.
  2. Each handover (distributor, retailer, sale) is mined into a new SHA-256 block.
  3. Consumers scan the barcode: no record, tampered chain, or cloned scans reveal a fake.
